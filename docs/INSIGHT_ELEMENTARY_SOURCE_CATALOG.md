### Workbook — Welcome

OCR review of the supplied Workbook scan confirms practice for:

- *be* affirmative/negative; countries and nationalities
- family vocabulary; possessive adjectives; plural nouns; demonstratives
- *have got*; object pronouns; articles
- days, months, seasons, numbers and dates
- WH-question words and a dialogue connected with “The world is a village”
- self-assessment against lesson learning objectives

## Important scope boundary

The Welcome unit is not equivalent to a generic “My World” A1 unit. The game module may use **My World** as its project narrative, but its teaching sequence and assessed language must map to Welcome A–D. Later-unit grammar such as Present Simple and *there is / there are* must not be inserted into Welcome assessments unless the supplied source explicitly places it there.
